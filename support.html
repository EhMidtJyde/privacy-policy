Padelico Support

Thanks for using Padelico! If you run into any issues or have questions about the app, here's how to get help:

Report an Issue

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/EhMidtJyde/privacy-policy/issues

Contact by Email

You can also email us at john.h.s@hotmail.com.

Common Questions

How do I reset a tournament? Open the reset button (top-right of the Match/Schedule/Standings screens).

Does Padelico collect my data? No. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
