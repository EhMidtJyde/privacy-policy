Padelico Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Padelico is a Padel tournament organizer app. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

Padelico does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All information you enter (such as player names and scores) is stored locally on your device only.

No Accounts, No Tracking

Padelico has no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet and does not communicate with any server.

Data Sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is shared with any third parties.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the email address shown on the App Store listing.
